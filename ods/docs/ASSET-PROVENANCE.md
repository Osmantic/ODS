# Dashboard artwork provenance

## Current wallpaper set: generated replacements

On 2026-09-24 UTC, the maintainer authorized treating the old wallpaper grants
as unavailable and replacing all 12 files. New artwork was created through
OpenAI image generation in Codex from generic text descriptions. The disputed
images were not supplied as generation inputs. Prompts requested original
abstract geometry or invented landscapes, with no logos, text, named artists
or copied commercial wallpaper designs. These are AI-generated illustrations,
including the photographic-style landscapes; they are not location photographs.

The new wallpaper files are distributed under the repository's
[Apache-2.0 license](../../LICENSE) to the extent applicable rights exist.
[OpenAI's output terms](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/#content)
address rights between OpenAI and its users; they do not guarantee exclusive
copyright or uniqueness. No rights in the former third-party images are claimed.

Production copies were center-fitted to the existing pixel dimensions and
encoded as JPEG at quality 90. Generation masters are smaller than some legacy
5K files; upscaling preserves compatibility, not additional native detail.
The new files total 9,437,208 bytes (9.00 MiB), compared with
39,225,046 bytes (37.41 MiB) previously. Old metadata is not carried forward.
Existing filenames and saved theme IDs remain compatibility keys; they no
longer identify the source, photographer, device or location. Visible names
now use Aurora Lake, Silver Fold and Dawn Clouds where needed.
