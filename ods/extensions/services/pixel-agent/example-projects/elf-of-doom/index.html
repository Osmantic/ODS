Elf of Doom

A dungeon crawler made with ODS on the RTX Spark

One elf. One bow. Endless brutes.
Run, draw, and loose arrows through great halls of the ashland — grab hearts to mend your wounds and quivers to keep your string singin', then loot, level, and outrun your way to the Cinderfall.

Break the Hollow Crown and the realms fold back on themselves: every circuit of six brings half again as many foes, hardier bosses and realm-6 reinforcements from the first hall onward. The quest never ends — it is only ever a matter of how far Shasan gets.

Controls

  • Arrow keys / WASD — run; you shoot the way you last ran
  • Hold Space to keep shooting — or tap J for single arrows
  • Hearts heal you, quivers refill your arrows — grab them between waves
  • 1, 2, 3 — choose a boon when you level up
  • Esc or P — pause · Enter — start / retry · M — mute
  • Clicking away from the game pauses it automatically
Clear every brute in the arena to open the way onward — 6 realms, each with its own boss, then Mount Cinderfall and the Hollow Crown. Fall the Crown and Continue into the next circuit: more brutes, tougher bosses, faster reinforcements — forever.
